create skeleton code utilizing the ursina engine for the following game. 
For context, I'm in a 2 day gamejam with the theme sacrifices must be made. 
I want to make a game where you are a conductor synchronizing a bunch of musicisans. 
Different groups of musciains are playing different instruments (specific instrument audio files), and you must tap space to ensure they all play on beat.  (Some audio tracks randomly slow or speed up). 
There will also be some musicians in each group that randomly play really badly / are bad actors, and you must hover over them and press q to take them away from affecting the entire orchestra.
For each musician taken away, the sound of that group decreases.
If you take too many musicians away, you lose the game.

here is what I want the game itself to look: there is a simple stage, and you (as a cube) are in front of that stage. there are multiple cubes at various places on the stage (imitating an orchestra) and you are having a half top down look on the entire field. 

Here are the files I would like and the function of each file.

main.py - 
loads the title screen, on clicking title screen enables the game scene.
update() - checks if basic gui is being interacted with (setting buttons, pause, etc)

level.py - game level.
gameDuration - how long the level lasts
beatAlign - constant value that reduces the offsynchronization by a certain amount.
offSetness - average offsetness of all the tracks combined from the target bpm.
concert[] - holds groupA, groupB, groupC
init(gameDuration) - calls reset - draws a flat ground as a scene, resets all the concert players to their respective position, and draws player in fron of the concert players
update() - handles game specific input space to align the music together.
- Also calls determineAudio() to determine groupA to C's audio settings
- calls victoryCondition()
victoryCondition() - checks if the offSetness is too great, if the gameDuration has passed and displays a simple victory screen, or if the total muscisians are too few.

musician.py - specific music person
badActor - T/F
position - x, y
collider - hit the stage and not clip through.
update() - rolls if the musician becomes a bad actor or not.

group.py - specific music group
musicians[] - how many specific musicians are in it.
init(# of musicians, width, length) - divides the amount of musicians evenly into the suggested area by dropping them in.
determineAudio(beatAlign) - checks the number of members to determine the audio volume, and calculate bad actor value of each musician with beatAlign to determine how off the speed of the audio should be. Returns the offsetness.

player.py - fixed camera looking down at the concert the a square
update() -  handles the game specific input q and does the action.
kill() - fires a raycast of the mouses direction and disables the entity hit.

imageManager.py - manages all images in a folder called images.

audioManager.py - manages each speciifc track of a song (drums, trumpet, bass, etc) in a folder called audios. Audio will be mp3 format.
boilerPlate functions to slow down / speed up.

Note - make all classes that use images draw their own fall back image if the image is not in file.
